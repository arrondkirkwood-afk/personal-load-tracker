    Dispatch and Earnings Analysis
    How to Read This Analysis

    Select the date range, review completed-load earnings, work time, goal achievement, dispatcher workload, then expand daily details.

    Completed-load pay measures the productivity of the loads assigned. Total estimated earnings includes every entered pay category. Goal percentages use eligible dispatched workdays as the denominator. Exact duty time comes only from Start Workday and End Workday. Completed-load pay per duty hour shows dispatched load pay produced per exact work hour. Non-load duty time is exact duty time not covered by valid load-cycle intervals.

    Missing information is excluded rather than guessed. Results describe measurable outcomes and do not establish a dispatcher’s intent. This tracker is not an official payroll system or ELD.

    Duty-Time Review

    This personal tracker does not distinguish every driving, on-duty, off-duty, sleeper, exception, or ELD status. Official ELD and company records control legal compliance determinations. A review threshold is not a finding of a legal violation.
